Fourier Transform

X(f) = integral from -infinity to +infinity of x(t) * e^(-j2*pi*f*t) dt

Euler's Identity

e^(j*pi) + 1 = 0

Gradient Descent Update Rule

theta_(n+1) = theta_n - alpha * gradient of J(theta_n)

where alpha is the learning rate and J is the cost function

Bayes' Theorem

P(A|B) = P(B|A) * P(A) / P(B)

Summation

S = sum from k=1 to N of (1/k^2) = pi^2 / 6

Quadratic Formula

x = (-b +/- sqrt(b^2 - 4ac)) / 2a
